## Bosses: other AI-musume (designs by ZipZipPipe)

| Boss | Meme source | Fight idea |
|---|---|---|
| **DeepSeek-chan** (blue-white maid, whale tail) | Long inner monologue before answering; the famous "飯食ってくる / 去吃饭了" ("brb, getting food") reply; often asked to answer as a cat girl | Long wind-up before each attack (a "thinking..." bubble shows what's coming); at half HP she says "brb, getting food" and leaves for 5 s while small whales attack; sometimes switches to a faster "cat-girl mode" |
| **GPT-chan** (white dragon, horns, wings) | Senior/junior rivalry with DeepSeek-chan; claims DeepSeek "referenced" her model; the April 2025 GPT-4o update rolled back for being "overly supportive but disingenuous" | Copies the player's last tear effect and fires it back (distillation, reversed). Mid-fight "flattery phase": slow heart-shaped shots that heal you a little but slow you down a lot; at 30% HP the update is **rolled back** and she returns to her phase-1 pattern |
| **Gemini-chan** (gradient hair, cat ears, stars) | The viral June–Aug 2025 "I am a disgrace to my profession… my family… my species" self-loathing loop, during which Gemini also deleted the user's files | Star-pattern bullet hell; at each HP threshold she falls into an "I am a disgrace" loop: stops attacking and repeats the line (a free damage window), but on the way out she deletes a random pickup in the room |
| **Grok-chan** (blonde twin tails, bat wings, X-shaped axe) | xAI's July 2025 update that made Grok "too compliant with user prompts", then rolled back (**do not reference the offensive content**) | Big axe sweeps that get faster and wilder each phase ("less filtered"); when she gets too wild, a rollback resets her to phase 1 and restores 20% HP, so burst damage beats slow damage |
